# ASR recording corpus

Thirty reference texts for real Telegram voice recordings. Record each entry once. Intonation, speed, room noise, microphone distance, and background voices are chosen during recording and logged separately.

Punctuation is editorial and does not need to be spoken. Keep Romanian diacritics, Cyrillic, names, numbers, corrections, filler words, and mixed-language switches in the reference transcript.
